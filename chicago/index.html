When Your System Fails in Chicago

Our typical arrival window in the area is typically within a day for urgent calls, and a dead AC in a heat wave or a furnace out on a freezing night is the kind of call we move to the front of the line.

Here's what we see most often when something breaks at home: warm air from the AC, a condenser that hums but won't start, a furnace that ignites and shuts off, a breaker that keeps tripping, an evaporator coil that has frozen, a condensate drain overflowing into the furnace cabinet, or a thermostat that has gone blank. A few minutes of caution while you wait for us can prevent a much larger repair. A frozen air conditioner should be shut off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw safely. Running it frozen can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. A short-cycling furnace, or any burning smell, means it's time to kill power at the furnace switch and call us immediately.

Keeping Your Chicago HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of summer.

Minor issues rarely stay small for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a miserable night.

Common HVAC Problems in Chicago

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. Those are early signs of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're an affordable repair. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
